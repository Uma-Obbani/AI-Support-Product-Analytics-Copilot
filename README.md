# AI Support & Product Analytics Copilot

AI-powered Data Product that combines customer support data, stakeholder requests, analytics, and AI-assisted insights to help Product Owners make evidence-based product decisions.

---

## 🎯 Product Vision

Help support and product teams turn customer and stakeholder signals into:

- actionable insights
- operational improvement opportunities
- data-driven product decisions
- measurable business outcomes

The product is designed as a **Data Product Owner decision-support system**, not simply an AI ticket classifier.

---

## 💼 Business Problem

Support organizations can receive large numbers of customer tickets while Product Owners receive requests from different business stakeholders.

Without a structured evidence-driven approach, it can be difficult to understand:

- Which customer problems occur most frequently
- Which problems create operational bottlenecks
- Which problems are increasing or decreasing
- Whether stakeholder requests are supported by customer evidence
- Whether an issue is product-related, technical, or process-related
- Whether automation could improve an operational process
- What business impact the problem creates
- Which opportunities should eventually enter the product backlog

This project connects these signals into one decision-support workflow.
