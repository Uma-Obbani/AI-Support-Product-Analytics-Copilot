# Product Owner Decision

## Problem
Payment

## Decision
INVESTIGATE

## Priority
HIGH

## Decision Rationale

Payment is currently the highest-priority problem for further
investigation based on the available evidence.

The evidence indicates:

- High customer support ticket volume
- High SLA breach rate
- High average resolution time
- Increasing recent ticket trend
- Existing stakeholder demand

## What Is Confirmed

- Customer support data shows significant operational pain.
- Payment is a significant support category.
- The available evidence supports further investigation.

## What Is Not Yet Confirmed

- Root cause
- Revenue impact
- Checkout conversion impact
- Customer lifetime-value impact
- Automation suitability

## Product Owner Decision

The Product Owner decides to investigate the Payment problem
before committing to a specific product solution.

## Next Investigation

The Product, Support, Engineering and Business stakeholders
should validate:

1. Root cause
2. Customer impact
3. Business/revenue impact
4. Technical causes
5. Process causes
6. Potential solution options
7. Expected effort and dependencies

## Important Principle

The prioritization model provides a recommendation.
The Product Owner makes the final product decision.
