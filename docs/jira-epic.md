# Jira Epic

## Epic Title

Improve Payment Experience and Reduce Payment-Related Support Friction

## Problem Statement

Payment-related customer support tickets show significant
operational pain based on the available evidence.

The current evidence indicates:

- High payment-related ticket volume
- High SLA breach rate
- High average resolution time
- Increasing recent ticket trend
- Existing stakeholder demand related to payment

However, the current evidence does not yet establish the root cause
or quantify the business/revenue impact.

## Product Objective

Investigate and address the underlying causes of payment-related
customer and support-process friction in order to improve the
payment experience and reduce avoidable support effort.

## Evidence

The current analysis identified:

- 63 payment-related support tickets
- 48 SLA breaches
- 76.19% SLA breach rate
- Approximately 1,970 minutes average resolution time
- Increasing latest-month ticket trend
- Stakeholder request related to payment

## Expected Outcome

The initiative should determine whether product, technical,
process, or automation improvements can reduce payment-related
customer friction and support effort.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Investigate payment-related customer problems
- Validate root causes
- Analyze customer and support impact
- Investigate technical causes
- Evaluate process improvements
- Evaluate potential product improvements
- Evaluate automation opportunities
- Define measurable success criteria

### Out of Scope

- Automatically implementing a solution
- Assuming payment problems cause revenue loss
- Assuming a specific root cause without evidence
- Automatically deciding the final product solution

## Dependencies

- Customer Support
- Product
- Engineering
- Business / Finance
- Relevant payment-system data

## Success Metrics

Potential metrics to validate:

- Payment-related ticket volume
- Payment-related SLA breach rate
- Average payment-ticket resolution time
- First-response time
- Payment failure rate
- Checkout conversion rate
- Customer impact
- Operational support effort

## Product Owner Decision

The Product Owner has prioritized Payment for further
investigation.

The specific solution will be determined only after validating
root cause, customer impact, business impact, technical feasibility,
and expected effort.

## Next Step

Break this Epic into User Stories with clear acceptance criteria.
