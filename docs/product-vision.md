# Product Vision

## Product Name

AI Support & Product Analytics Copilot

## Vision

Help support and product teams turn support data into intelligent
triage, actionable product insights, and measurable improvements.

## Product Goal

Build an AI-assisted platform that:

1. Classifies incoming support tickets.
2. Prioritizes tickets based on customer and business impact.
3. Identifies support-process bottlenecks.
4. Detects automation opportunities.
5. Provides a semantic analytics layer for KPI analysis.
6. Generates evidence-based product insights.
7. Helps Product Owners convert insights into product requirements.

## Target Users

- Product Owners
- Product Managers
- Support Managers
- Support Operations Teams
- Data Analysts

## Core Product Principle

AI should support Product Owner decision-making rather than
replace human decision-making.

The system should clearly distinguish:

- Facts
- Evidence
- Hypotheses
- Recommendations
- Product decisions
