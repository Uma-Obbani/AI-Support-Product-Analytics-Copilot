# Problem Statement

## Problem

Support teams can receive large numbers of customer tickets.
Manual triage and prioritization can consume significant time,
while recurring ticket patterns and process bottlenecks may not
be immediately visible.

Product teams also need reliable analytics to understand:

- What problems customers are experiencing
- Which problems have the greatest business impact
- Where support processes are inefficient
- Which processes could be automated
- Which product improvements should be investigated

## Current Challenges

1. Manual ticket classification
2. Manual ticket prioritization
3. Difficulty identifying recurring bottlenecks
4. Limited visibility into SLA risks
5. Time-consuming KPI analysis
6. Separation between support data and product decision-making

## Opportunity

Create a unified AI-assisted workflow that connects:

Support Tickets
→ Analytics
→ Bottleneck Detection
→ Product Insights
→ Product Decisions
